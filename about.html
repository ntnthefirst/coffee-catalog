The numbers

Good to know

  • One espresso shot is 25 ml, a double is 50 ml. On the Dedica use 8 g (single) and 15 g (double) in the double-wall baskets.

  • Caffeine is an estimate of about 63 mg per shot. Real values depend on beans, roast and dose.

  • One syrup pump is taken as 10 ml. Pumps differ per bottle, so measure yours once.

  • Only espresso, ristretto and lungo have an official definition. Milk drinks vary per café, so they follow published ratios.

  • Cup clearance, tank size and hot-water function are not listed because they could not be confirmed. Check your manual.

  • Drawings are diagrams generated from each recipe’s amounts, not photos.
