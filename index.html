    The strongest coffees

    Two ways to be “strong”: caffeine per cup (the kick) and intensity (how concentrated the coffee taste is per 100 ml).

      How is this calculated?

      Caffeine ≈ 63 mg per espresso shot (a commonly cited figure — real values vary with bean, roast and dose). Intensity = caffeine ÷ total drink volume, shown as mg per 100 ml and grouped into five levels. Ice is not counted as volume. Decaf shots are counted as ~4 mg.
